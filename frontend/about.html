Our Story

AF Digital Hub was founded by Adonle Fameye — Founder, Coach, and Affiliate Marketer — to help creators and businesses find each other and get real campaigns done.

After years spent building digital products and coaching others through affiliate marketing, Adonle saw a gap: businesses struggled to find the right creators for their campaigns, and creators struggled to find real, paying opportunities that matched their audience.

This marketplace exists to close that gap — giving businesses a straightforward way to post campaigns, and giving creators a fair, transparent way to apply, get paid, and build their portfolio.
