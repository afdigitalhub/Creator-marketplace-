Adonle Fameye is the founder of AF Digital Hub, a digital marketplace built to give creators, businesses, and everyday people a real place to learn, sell, and grow online.

Based in Ghana, Adonle built AF Digital Hub using his phone and laptop, without shortcuts, driven by a clear belief that opportunity shouldn't depend on where you start from. Every eBook in the Shop was written by him personally, and every part of the platform was built through patience, persistence, and hands-on work.

AF Digital Hub exists because Adonle wanted to build something real: a place where people can access practical knowledge, sell digital products, find genuine business opportunities, and build something of their own, regardless of their background or resources.

His goal is simple: to make AF Digital Hub a trusted platform that people across Ghana and beyond can rely on to learn, earn, and build a better future.

Our Story

AF Digital Hub was founded by Adonle Fameye — Founder, Coach, and Affiliate Marketer — to help creators and businesses find each other and get real campaigns done.

After years spent building digital products and coaching others through affiliate marketing, Adonle saw a gap: businesses struggled to find the right creators for their campaigns, and creators struggled to find real, paying opportunities that matched their audience.

This marketplace exists to close that gap — giving businesses a straightforward way to post campaigns, and giving creators a fair, transparent way to apply, get paid, and build their portfolio.
