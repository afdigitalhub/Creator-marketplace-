Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 2026

Overview

AF Digital Hub ("we", "us", "our") operates a marketplace connecting creators and businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and role (creator or business) when you register.
  • Campaign data: campaign details you post as a business, or pitches and proposals you submit as a creator.
  • Communications: messages, applications, and reviews exchanged through the platform.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as device type and general activity on the platform, used to keep the service running smoothly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect creators with relevant campaigns and businesses with relevant creators.
  • To process applications, deliverables, payouts, and reviews between users.
  • To send you notifications about activity relevant to your account.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information you choose to share as part of a campaign (such as your name and email in an application) is visible to the other party involved in that campaign, as needed to complete the collaboration.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted password storage and secure authentication. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

Your Choices

You can update your profile information at any time. If you'd like your account or data removed, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of AF Digital Hub after changes means you accept the updated policy.
